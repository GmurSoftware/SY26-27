Avatar: The Way of Water

Directed by James Cameron

Is a 2022 American Epic Science Fiction Film directed by James Cameron, and written by Cameron, Rick Jaffa and Amanda Silver. It is the second installment in the Avatar Film Series and the sequel to Avatar (2009) The plot follows Jake Sully, a human-Na'vi hybrid, and his family on the habitable moon Pandora.
